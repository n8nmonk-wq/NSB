Case Study, Institution & Model

Industry work grouped by purpose.

The Zandu turnaround, CRIA consultation route, and MANTHAN model are now merged as one industry section: revive credibility, create a dedicated advisory path, and give product strategy a disciplined Ayurvedic method.

Zandu, CRIA, and MANTHAN as one industry route.

These are no longer treated as three independent sections. They are combined as a progression from corporate recovery to institutional consultation and strategic churning.

  • Zandu turnaround A period of stagnation was answered with renewed product credibility, operational direction, clinical validation, and record double-digit growth.
  • CRIA consultation route A dedicated advisory path for Ayurvedic industry, product strategy, standards, validation, and global communication.
  • MANTHAN model A disciplined process that weighs therapeutic credibility, consumer need, research evidence, manufacturing standards, and sector responsibility before strategy is recommended.

From patient trust to sector confidence.

The industry work stays rooted in patients and practitioners first. Product growth is framed as the outcome of clinical truth, quality discipline, and responsible public communication.

  • Patient and practitioner confidence Commercial decisions begin with therapeutic credibility rather than market opportunity alone.
  • Product discipline Formulation clarity, quality, documentation, and transparent communication make Ayurveda scalable.
  • Durable sector value Institutions are guided toward decisions that strengthen public trust and long-term category value.
