Corporate Case Study

The Zandu Turnaround

When Dr. Bhatt assumed the role of CEO at Zandu Pharmaceuticals in 2003, the company was facing over a decade of stagnation. Utilizing his deep clinical knowledge and massive structural vision, he initiated a complete overhaul of their product pipeline and operational strategies.

By enforcing rigorous clinical validations—including the famous 55 coordinated studies—he not only revitalized the brand's legacy but drove it to achieve record-breaking double-digit growth, setting a new gold standard for Ayurvedic manufacturing worldwide.
