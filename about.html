Full Arc

From clinical roots to institutional leadership.

Dr. Bhatt began with teaching and clinical work in Ayurveda, including postgraduate training and physician roles connected with Podar institutions and later K.G.M. Punarvasu Ayurvedic College and Hospital.

He then moved into research, product development, standardization, and pharmaceutical industry leadership. His work at Zandu Pharmaceuticals, Ajanta Pharma, Bio-Ved, Kerala Ayurveda, and CRIA connected Ayurvedic knowledge with organizational systems, scientific standards, and product credibility.

In academic and public institutions, he served in roles including Director Research in Ayurveda at Bharati Vidyapeeth and National Professor / Visiting Scientist at Haffkine Institute, participating in governance, national research advisory panels, and international delegations.
