Legacy of Healing

A Legacy of Healing

Dr. Narendra S. Bhatt, M.D. Ayu. & B.Sc. from the University of Bombay, is a 4th generation Ayurvedic physician with 40 years of teaching, clinical, research, and pharmaceutical experience. Former CEO of Zandu Pharmaceuticals. Currently Adjunct Professor & Director for Research at College of Ayurveda, Bharati Vidyapeeth University, Pune.

Professional Experience

Dr. Bhatt possesses over 40 years of multifaceted experience spanning Academic Teaching, Clinical Practice, Advanced Research, and Pharmaceutical Manufacturing. He currently serves as Adjunct Professor & Director for Research at the College of Ayurveda, Bharati Vidyapeeth University, Pune.

  • Zandu Pharmaceuticals (2003–2007): Served as CEO, reversing a decade-long stagnation to achieve record-breaking double-digit growth and spearheading robust clinical validations.
  • Ajanta Pharma (1993–1997): Acted as Vice President, modernizing product pipelines for modern pharmacological compliance.
  • Consultancy (1982–1987): Honorary Advisor for Ranbaxy and Procter & Gamble.

Honors & Awards

Over four decades, Dr. Bhatt has received prestigious national and international awards recognizing his commitment to scientific validation of Ayurveda and relentless push for industry standardization.

Most notably, his coordination of 55 clinical studies during his tenure at Zandu directly contributed to a joint Indian Patent for the treatment of Parkinson's Disease — a landmark achievement in Ayurvedic neurology.

He is frequently cited in leading pharmacognosy textbooks and has been a staple keynote speaker at global conferences, bringing unprecedented academic rigor to traditional healing arts.

Key Associations & CRIA

Dr. Bhatt has been repeatedly sought out for his unmatched expertise, acting as crucial consultant for the World Health Organization (WHO) in Geneva, and the Global Research Alliance under the World Bank.

At the core of his current academic efforts is his tie to the foundation of CRIA (Centre for Research and Innovation in Ayurveda) — a premier initiative designed to foster high-level scientific research, establish rigorous clinical standards, and promote Ayurvedic innovation globally.

Through CRIA and his international advisory roles, Dr. Bhatt is actively working to dismantle the silos between Western allopathy and Eastern holistic medicine.

Heritage

The Fourth Generation Lineage

The roots of Dr. Bhatt's practice run deep. Inheriting an uninterrupted legacy of healing, he represents the fourth consecutive generation of Ayurvedic physicians in his family.

This ancestral wisdom was primarily passed down through his father, Vaidya D. S. Antarkar, a luminary whose clinical acumen laid the foundational ethos of treating the patient as a complete entity rather than a collection of symptoms.
