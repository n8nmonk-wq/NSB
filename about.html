Full arc

From clinical Ayurveda to institutional leadership.


Dr. Bhatt began with teaching and clinical work in Ayurveda, including postgraduate training and physician roles connected with Podar institutions and later K.G.M. Punarvasu Ayurvedic College and Hospital.

He then moved into research, product development, standardization, and pharmaceutical industry leadership. His work at Zandu Pharmaceuticals, Ajanta Pharma, Bio-Ved, Kerala Ayurveda, and CRIA connected Ayurvedic knowledge with organizational systems and product credibility.

In academic and public institutions, he served in roles including Director Research in Ayurveda at Bharati Vidyapeeth and National Professor / Visiting Scientist at Haffkine Institute. His contributions include expert committee work, research advisory roles, international delegations, public lectures, publications, edited volumes, and a joint patent.

The through-line

Clinical depth became research method. Research method became industry discipline. Industry discipline became institutional and policy contribution.

Ayurveda is presented here not as nostalgia, but as a living professional discipline shaped through care, evidence, enterprise, and public responsibility.
