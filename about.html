A Legacy of Healing

Dr. Narendra S. Bhatt, M.D. Ayu. & B.Sc. from the University of Bombay, is a 4th generation Ayurvedic physician with 40 years of teaching, clinical, research, and pharmaceutical experience. He is a former CEO of Zandu Pharmaceuticals and currently serves as Adjunct Professor & Director for Research at College of Ayurveda, Bharati Vidyapeeth University, Pune.

Professional Experience

Dr. Bhatt possesses over 40 years of multifaceted experience spanning Academic Teaching, Clinical Practice, Advanced Research, and Pharmaceutical Manufacturing. He currently serves as an Adjunct Professor & Director for Research at the College of Ayurveda, Bharati Vidyapeeth University, Pune, actively shaping the next generation of practitioners.

  • Zandu Pharmaceuticals (2003-2007): Served as CEO, reversing a decade-long stagnation to achieve record-breaking double-digit growth and spearheading robust clinical validations for legacy products.
  • Ajanta Pharma (1993-1997): Acted as Vice President, modernizing product pipelines and bringing traditional Ayurvedic formulations into modern pharmacological compliance.
  • Consultancy (1982-1987): Acted as a trusted Honorary Advisor for massive consumer and pharmaceutical brands including Ranbaxy and Procter & Gamble.

Honors & Awards

Over the span of his four-decade career, Dr. Bhatt has been the recipient of several prestigious national and international awards. These accolades recognize his lifetime commitment to the scientific validation of Ayurveda and his relentless push for industry standardization.

His dedication to bridging the gap between ancient remedies and modern medicine has been celebrated by governing medical bodies across the globe. Most notably, his groundbreaking coordination of 55 clinical studies during his tenure at Zandu directly contributed to the securing of a joint Indian Patent for the treatment of Parkinson's Disease—a landmark achievement in Ayurvedic neurology.

Beyond patents, he is frequently cited in leading pharmacognosy textbooks and has been a staple keynote speaker at global conferences, bringing unprecedented academic rigor to traditional healing arts.

Key Associations & CRIA

Dr. Bhatt's influence extends far beyond the clinic and the laboratory; he is a foundational figure in major Ayurvedic and integrative medicine associations globally. He has been repeatedly sought out for his unmatched expertise, acting as a crucial consultant for the World Health Organization (WHO) in Geneva, and the Global Research Alliance under the World Bank.

At the core of his current academic and organizational efforts is his intrinsic tie to the foundation of CRIA (Centre for Research and Innovation in Ayurveda). CRIA is a premier initiative designed to foster high-level scientific research, establish rigorous clinical standards, and promote Ayurvedic innovation on a global scale.

Through CRIA and his international advisory roles, Dr. Bhatt is actively working to dismantle the silos between Western allopathy and Eastern holistic medicine, ensuring Ayurveda has a protected, respected seat at the global healthcare table.

The Fourth Generation Lineage

The roots of Dr. Bhatt's practice run deep. Inheriting an uninterrupted legacy of healing, he represents the fourth consecutive generation of Ayurvedic physicians in his family.

This ancestral wisdom was primarily passed down and formalized through his father, Vaidya D. S. Antarkar, a luminary whose clinical acumen laid the foundational ethos of Dr. Bhatt's modern practice—an ethos that insists on treating the patient as a complete entity rather than a collection of symptoms.
